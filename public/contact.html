QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel or change my booking?

At the moment NavyaFlix is a demo project. In a live system you would be able to cancel up to 2 hours before the show and receive a full refund.

How many seats can I book at once?

You can book up to 8 seats per booking. For group bookings of more than 8, please contact us directly.

What payment methods are accepted?

This is a demo project, so no real payment is processed. In production, we would support UPI, credit / debit cards and net banking.

Where can I find my booking confirmation?

All confirmed bookings appear on the My Bookings page immediately after checkout.

Which theatres does NavyaFlix cover?

NavyaFlix lists shows across multiple partner theatres. Visit the Book Tickets page to see all available venues and showtimes.
